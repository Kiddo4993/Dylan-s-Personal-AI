# Code of conduct

Fixel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and remember that many people here are students who are new to open source. Critique code, not people. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions, or anywhere else the project lives.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub profile linked from this repository rather than in a public thread. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules, as the Covenant describes.
